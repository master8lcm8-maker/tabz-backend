How 8TABZ works

Every role gets a clear path. Every paid value gets a visible state. No feature should stay buried in code.

Discover

Users discover venues, browse venues, bars, clubs, stores, menus, products, and public venue profiles.

Send / Claim / Buy

Users send gifts, users claim FreeBoard items, and users buy/order items from venue menus and stores.

Redeem

Staff confirms, redeems, or rejects QR and entitlement states at the venue.

Audit

Admin HQ audits users, venues, wallets, ledger/reconciliation, refunds/disputes, payouts, risk, and final resolution.
