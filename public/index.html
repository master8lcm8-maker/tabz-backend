How 8TABZ works

Every role gets a clear path. Every paid value gets a visible state. No feature should stay buried in code.

Discover

Users discover venues, browse venues, bars, clubs, stores, menus, products, and public venue profiles.

Send / Claim / Buy

Users send gifts, users claim FreeBoard items, and users buy/order items from venue menus and stores.

Redeem

Staff confirms, redeems, or rejects QR and entitlement states at the venue.

Audit

Admin HQ audits users, venues, wallets, ledger/reconciliation, refunds/disputes, payouts, risk, and final resolution.

QR redemption, claims, and wallet value

8TABZ redemption uses protected live routes. Staff and signed-in users must authenticate before redeeming, claiming, or viewing wallet value.

Staff redeem

Redeem drink by code

Protected route: POST /drinks/redeem. Staff redeems valid drink gift codes; non-staff roles are blocked.

FreeBoard claim

Claim sponsored drops

Protected routes: POST /freeboard/claim and GET /freeboard/my. Users claim by code and track their created or claimed drops.

Wallet value

Track balance and cashout rules

Protected wallet routes: GET /wallet/summary, POST /wallet/transfer, and POST /wallet/cashout.
