8TABZ Privacy Policy

8TABZ collects and processes account, profile, transaction, and app usage data required to operate venue engagement, wallet, ordering, security, and support features.

8TABZ may collect identifiers such as email address, account role, device and usage data, and transaction records necessary for service delivery, fraud prevention, and compliance.

8TABZ uses this information to provide services, authenticate users, process transactions, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and improve the product.

8TABZ may share data with service providers only as needed to support hosting, payments, authentication, communications, analytics, and customer support.

Users may request account deletion, subject to legal, fraud-prevention, accounting, and compliance retention requirements.

For privacy questions, contact 8TABZ support.
