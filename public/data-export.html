8TABZ Data Export

This page explains how users can request access to account-related data or ask for data export support.

What can be requested

  • Account profile information connected to your 8TABZ account.
  • Support information needed to review wallet, gift, FreeBoard, order, payout, or redemption history.
  • Privacy or account deletion support information when applicable.

How to request data help

Email master8lcm8@gmail.com with “Data Export Request” in the subject line.

Verification

8TABZ may need to verify the requesting account before sharing or exporting account-related information.
